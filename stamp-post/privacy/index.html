Stamp Post

Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026

Stamp Post needs no account. We, Kaevo Labs, do not collect your name, email or any other personal data on our own servers, because the game has none. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below.

Who we are

Stamp Post is made by Kaevo Labs. For any privacy question, write to kaevolabs@gmail.com.

Data stored on your phone

Your progress, stars, settings, the player name and the country you choose stay on your device. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app or clearing its data in Android settings removes all of it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads: optional rewarded ads you choose to watch, and an occasional full-screen ad between stamps. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language),
  • how you interact with ads.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy options. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Google's own use of this data is explained at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings under Google › Ads.

Purchases

The one-time "Remove ads" purchase is handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details; the game only learns from Google Play whether you own the purchase, so it can turn ads off.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled on your phone. No data leaves your device for it, and you can turn it off in the game's settings.

Children

Stamp Post is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data. The only third parties involved are Google AdMob and Google Play, as described above.

Changes

If the game gains online features, such as leaderboards, we will update this policy before they go live and change the date above.
